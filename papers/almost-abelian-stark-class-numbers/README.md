**Truncated higher Stark elements and p-adic class-number divisibility for almost abelian extensions of imaginary quadratic fields.**

An *almost abelian datum* is a finite abelian extension \(F/k\) with \(k\) imaginary quadratic. The extension \(F/\mathbb{Q}\) need not be Galois. Write \(G=\mathrm{Gal}(F/k)\), \(E_F=\mathcal{O}_F^\times/\mu(F)\), and \(\mathfrak{z}(F/k)=w_k/w_F\) for the annihilator index of the roots of unity.

## Main statements

**Stark–Dirichlet index.** The regulator lattice of \(E_F\) and the lattice generated by \(\prod_{\chi\neq 1} L'(0,\chi)\) in the determinant line of the augmentation kernel have generalized index
\[
\frac{h_F}{h_k}\cdot\mathfrak{z}(F/k)\in\mathbb{Q}_{>0}.
\]

**p-adic higher Stark lattices.** If \(p\nmid |G|\), then \(\mathbb{Z}_p[G]\) is a product of unramified discrete valuation rings. For each non-trivial idempotent, the corresponding piece of the \(S\)-unit lattice is free of rank equal to the order of vanishing of the truncated Artin \(L\)-function, and its determinant is a free rank-one higher Stark module. If a ramified place is omitted and Frobenius fails to generate the inertia quotient, that rank is too small for the ramification-complete leading value, so no integral higher Stark element of the smaller level maps to it.

**Obstruction and divisibility.** The same rank gap is the order obstruction. Under the non-decomposition hypothesis at every ramified place, truncating by those places multiplies the product of derivatives by the integer \(\prod_v |G/I_v|\). For \(p\nmid |G|\) this integer is a \(p\)-unit, and
\[
v_p(h_F/h_k)=\sum_{\psi\neq 1} v_p\bigl(|e_\psi(\mathrm{Cl}_F\otimes\mathbb{Z}_p)|\bigr)\ge 0,
\]
which equals the \(p\)-valuation of the Stark–Dirichlet index after the correction \(v_p(w_F)-v_p(w_k)\).

The note uses the analytic class number formula and Hecke's functional equation as inputs. Individual ratios \(L'(0,\chi)/R(\chi)\) are not claimed to be algebraic; only the full product, divided by the Dirichlet regulator, is proved rational.

## Check

```bash
gp -q examples/verify-index.gp
```

The script compares the identity on three quadratic extensions. In each case the ratio of the analytic side to \((h_F/h_k)(w_k/w_F)R_F\) equals \(1\) at 30 decimal places. One of the examples has \(v_2(h_F)<v_2(h_k)\) with \(|G|=2\), so the restriction \(p\nmid |G|\) in the divisibility is sharp.
